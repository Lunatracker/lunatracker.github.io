Questions from teens (and parents)

Is my data private?

Luna will never sell your data or show you ads. You can also turn on an optional passcode so the app stays private even if someone else picks up your phone.

Who is Luna for?

Luna is built for teens, tweens, and anyone who is just starting out their period. If other period apps feel like they weren't made for you, this one was.

How does Luna predict my period?

Teen cycles are often still settling into a rhythm, so Luna uses predictions designed for teen bodies. Log your period, and Luna learns your pattern and gives you a gentle heads-up — including prep days — before the next one starts.

Where can I download Luna?

Luna is available on the App Store for iPhone and on Google Play for Android.
