What to Put in a Period Kit for School

A small bag that makes periods at school way less stressful.

2 min read · October 18, 2026

Getting your period at school can feel stressful, especially when you aren't expecting it. A small period kit in your backpack or locker means you're ready no matter what. Here's what to put in it.

The period kit checklist

  • 2 to 3 pads or tampons (or both, if you use both). Pads are a good choice if you're just starting.
  • A few panty liners for light days or spotting
  • A spare pair of underwear in a zip bag
  • Wipes to freshen up
  • A small zip bag for anything that gets messy
  • A hair tie or jacket to tie around your waist if you leak
  • Pain relief, only if your school allows it and a parent says it's okay. Many schools keep medicine with the nurse.

Pick the right bag

Any small pouch works: a pencil case, a makeup bag, or a zip pouch. Pick one that's easy to grab and carry to the bathroom without anyone knowing what's inside. Some people keep one in their backpack and a second one in their locker or sports bag.

What if you forget your kit?

It happens to everyone. Here are some options:

  • Ask the school nurse or front office. They almost always have pads.
  • Ask a friend or a teacher you trust. You can keep it quick: "Do you have a pad?"
  • Fold toilet paper into a thick strip and line your underwear with it until you can get a pad.
  • Check if your school bathrooms have a pad machine or basket.

What to do if you leak

Leaks are really common, especially when your periods aren't regular yet. Tie a jacket or sweater around your waist, head to the bathroom or nurse, and change into your spare underwear. Cold water works best for rinsing blood out of fabric. If you need to go home to change, the nurse can usually call a parent.

Know when to pack it

The hardest part of a teen period is not knowing when it's coming. Tracking helps. Luna shows prep days on the calendar before your period is expected, and can send a discreet reminder that just says "Event tomorrow," so you know it's time to restock your kit.
