Spotting vs. Period: How to Tell the Difference

Light bleeding between periods is common for teens. Here's what it means.

2 min read · October 18, 2026

Found a few drops of blood in your underwear, but it's not time for your period? That's probably spotting. It's very common, especially in the first couple of years after periods start.

What is spotting?

Spotting is very light bleeding that happens outside of your regular period. It might just be a few drops, or a pink or brown smear when you wipe. It usually doesn't need a pad, though a panty liner can help.

Spotting vs. period: the differences

  • Amount: Spotting is a few drops. A period is enough blood that you need a pad, tampon, cup or period underwear.
  • Color: Spotting is often light pink or brown. Period blood is usually red, at least on the heaviest days.
  • How long: Spotting usually lasts a few hours to a day or two. A period usually lasts 2 to 7 days.
  • Other symptoms: Periods often come with cramps, bloating or sore breasts. Spotting often doesn't.

Why teens spot

In your first few years of having periods, your hormones are still finding a rhythm. That can lead to:

  • Spotting a day or two before your period starts
  • Spotting for a day or two after your period ends
  • Light bleeding in the middle of your cycle, around ovulation
  • A "period" that's so light it's basically spotting

Other things that can cause spotting include stress, big changes in exercise, travel, being sick, or starting hormonal birth control.

When to talk to a doctor

Spotting is usually nothing to worry about, but check in with a doctor if:

  • You spot often, or for many days in a row
  • Spotting comes with strong pain, fever, or unusual-smelling discharge
  • You spot after sex
  • You've been regular for a while and suddenly start spotting a lot

Logging spotting

It's helpful to keep track of spotting days separately from period days. That way you and your doctor can see the real pattern. In Luna, you can log your flow as spotting, light, medium or heavy for any day.
